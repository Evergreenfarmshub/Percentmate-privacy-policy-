PercentMate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PercentMate handles information when you use the PercentMate percentage calculator application.

PercentMate is designed to provide percentage calculations and related mathematical tools directly on your device.

Developer: PERCENTMATE DEVELOPERS

2. Information We Collect

PercentMate does not require account registration and, as configured, does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.

Values entered into the calculator are processed locally on your device to produce calculation results.

The app does not request personal information such as your name, phone number, address or email address to provide its core functionality.

Please do not enter confidential personal information into calculation fields.

3. Calculation History and Local Storage

If you use the calculation history feature, your previous calculations may be saved locally on your device.

This information is used to let you review and reuse previous calculations. The locally stored history is not transmitted to our servers.

You can clear your calculation history using the available in-app controls. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and Android settings.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

PercentMate is designed to operate without an internet connection.

As configured, the app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking SDKs or developer-operated servers.

If these services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declarations will be updated as necessary.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information because the app, as configured, does not collect or transmit personal information.

If you choose to use your device's copy or sharing functionality, the information you select may be transferred to another application. That transfer is initiated by you and is subject to the receiving application's privacy practices.

6. Data Security

Calculation data is processed and stored locally on your device.

You are responsible for securing access to your device. The security of locally stored information may depend on your device's operating system, security settings and other applications installed on it.

7. Children's Privacy

PercentMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

The app does not require users to provide personal information to perform its core calculations.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Calculation history remains on your device until you delete it through the app's available controls, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app.

We do not maintain a remote database of calculation history.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published at the privacy policy address associated with the app's Google Play listing.
